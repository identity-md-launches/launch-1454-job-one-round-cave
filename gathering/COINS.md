The four line tools and the Anchor bridge work with public Ethereum evidence or
entirely offline. Their usefulness does not depend on minting, pricing, holding,
or paying with another asset. Real ZTO is the default token in Line 4 and is
used in Line 1 and Line 3 examples; IMD remains the second supported token where
the source tools offer it. Neither requires a new currency or a Uniswap v4 hook.

No coin request, `coin-order.json`, contract deployment order, request-check
POST, wallet, key, signing, payment, or deployment is proposed.
